Blockli apps support push notifications on both iOS and Android. Notifications can include **images** alongside the title and message.

To enable push notifications, you need to upload your Firebase and APNs credentials in Studio first. See [iOS Credentials](/studio/credentials-ios) and [Android Credentials](/studio/credentials-android).

## Manual notifications

You can send a push notification to any audience directly from Studio. Choose who receives it, write your message, optionally attach an image, and send.

This is useful for announcements, promotions, or any message you want to push to a specific group of members.

## Automatic notifications

Automatic notifications are triggered by activity on your platform — no manual action needed. These include:

- **BuddyBoss platform notifications** — new messages, group invites, connection requests, activity mentions, comments, forum replies, and more
- **Custom notifications** — any additional notification events integrated into the plugin

Members can manage which notifications they receive from the notification preferences screen in the app.
