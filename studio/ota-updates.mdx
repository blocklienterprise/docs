An On-Air Release lets you update your live app instantly — no new build, no App Store review. When you publish from Studio, the changes go out to all your members on their next app launch.

## What you can update with an On-Air Release

- Navigation structure and tab bar
- Colors and theme
- Enabled modules and feature flags
- App page content

## What requires a new build

Some changes are baked into the app binary and can't be updated over the air:

- App icon and splash screen
- New native features or integrations

## How to publish

Click **Publish** in Studio. The update is pushed immediately and takes effect the next time a member opens the app.
