If your site already has an app built with BuddyBoss App, you don't start from scratch. Blockli brings your setup across and ships as an **update to the app your members already have**, under the same App Store and Google Play listings.

Starting without a BuddyBoss app? Follow the [Quickstart](/quickstart) instead.

<Info>
  Leave your BuddyBoss App setup in place while you move. The migration reads from it and never changes it.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the Blockli plugin">
    Download the **Blockli Mobile App** plugin zip from your Studio account, then in WordPress go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**, upload it and click **Activate**. A **Blockli App** menu appears in your WordPress sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your app in Studio and connect your site">
    In WordPress, go to **Blockli App → Overview** and click **Connect**. Studio opens: create your app there (or pick one you already made) and your site is connected. See [Creating Your First App](/studio/create-app) for more detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your setup across">
    In WordPress, go to **Blockli App → Tools → BuddyBoss App Migration**. Click **Preview everything** to see what each step would bring over, then **Migrate** step by step or **Migrate everything**.

    This moves your branding, colours and font, app settings, app pages, in-app products, access rules, menus, members' existing purchases and bookmarks. Every step can be undone.

    Run **Existing purchases** last, after **In-app products** and **Access control**, so members keep exactly what they paid for. The full list of steps is in [BuddyBoss App Migration](/plugin/buddyboss-migration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move your store credentials">
    On the same migration page, under **Store credentials for Blockli Studio**, click **Download migration file** and copy the code that's shown. It appears only once.

    In Studio, open your app's **Store Setup → Credentials**, choose **Moving from BuddyBoss App?**, upload the file and enter the code. Studio shows what it will fill in before saving: your App Store Connect key, Firebase and Google Play files, and your Android upload keystore.

    <Warning>
      Keep the code separate from the file. Anyone with both can open your store credentials.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your bundle identifiers">
    In Studio, under **Moving from BuddyBoss App?**, click **Get my BuddyBoss App identifiers**, then **Use these identifiers**, and save.

    Using the same iOS bundle identifier and Android package name is what makes your Blockli app an update to your existing app. Members keep their subscriptions and keep getting notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a Preview build">
    Go to **Build**, click **New Build** and choose the **Preview** profile. Install it on your phone and check your pages, menus, sign-in and purchases. A Preview app installs alongside your live app, so members aren't affected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release the update">
    When you're happy, start a **Release** build and submit it from Studio with **Submit to Store**. Give it a version number higher than the version of your BuddyBoss app that's in the stores now: Apple and Google only accept updates with a higher version.

    Members get Blockli as a normal app update. Links in old notifications and shared links to BuddyBoss App pages keep working and open the matching Blockli page or screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>
