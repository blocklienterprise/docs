Members log in to your Blockli app using the same credentials they use on your WordPress site.

## WordPress login

Members sign in with their WordPress username and password. No additional setup is needed — if they have an account on your site, they can log in to the app.

## Social login

If you have social login enabled on your site, members can also sign in with:

- **Facebook**
- **Google**
- **Apple**

Social login buttons appear automatically on the app's sign-in screen when the corresponding providers are configured on your WordPress site.
