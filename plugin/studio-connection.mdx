Connecting your WordPress site to Studio links your community content to your app. The connection is started from your WordPress admin.

## Connect your site

1. In your WordPress admin, go to **Blockli Mobile App → Overview** and click **Connect**.
2. You will be redirected to Studio automatically.
3. Select the app you want to connect to this site, or create a new one.
4. The connection is confirmed and your WordPress URL is set up in Studio.

Once connected, the plugin Overview page shows a **Connected** badge with your app name.

## Reconnecting

If you migrate your site to a new domain or need to refresh the connection:

1. In your WordPress admin, go to **Blockli Mobile App → Overview** and click **Reconnect**.
2. You will be redirected to Studio to reauthorize. Existing app sessions are not affected.

## Troubleshooting

**Could not reach site**

- Make sure pretty permalinks are enabled: **Settings → Permalinks** — any structure except Plain works.
- Check that a security plugin such as Wordfence or iThemes Security is not blocking REST API requests.
- Confirm your site URL is publicly reachable.

**Plugin not detected after connecting**

- Check that the plugin is activated, not just installed, under **Plugins** in your WordPress admin.
