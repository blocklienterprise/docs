Blockli connects three pieces:

| Component | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **WordPress Plugin** | Installed on your site. Exposes your community data, handles authentication, push notifications, and connects your site to Studio. |
| **Studio Cloud** | The web dashboard where you configure, build, and publish your app. Controls branding, navigation, app store credentials, and OTA updates. |
| **Mobile App** | The white-label React Native app delivered to your users on iOS and Android. Powered by your WordPress site, configured by Studio. |

An optional **SDK** is available for custom development and theming — documented separately.

## How it works

```text
WordPress site                    Studio Cloud                 App stores
─────────────────                 ────────────                 ──────────
BuddyBoss + Plugin  ──────────►  Configure app  ──────────►  iOS + Android
(data + auth)        sync         Build binary   publish       (users)
                                  OTA updates ──────────────► (live)
```

1. **Install the plugin** on your WordPress / BuddyBoss site. It creates the REST API endpoints and settings that Studio reads.
2. **Connect Studio** to your site. Studio syncs your site's capabilities (which community providers are active, what modules are enabled) and uses that to generate the app configuration.
3. **Configure your app** in Studio: branding, navigation, screens, and feature flags.
4. **Add credentials** for the iOS App Store and Google Play.
5. **Trigger a build** via EAS. Studio handles the Expo build pipeline and code-signing.
6. **Publish** your app to the App Store and Google Play.
7. **Push OTA updates** any time you change configuration or content — no new App Store release required.

## Requirements

- WordPress 5.8\+, PHP 7.4\+
- **BuddyBoss Platform** (free) installed and activated — this is the community layer the app is built on top of
- A [Blockli Studio](https://blockli-studio-cloud.onrender.com) account
- Apple Developer account (for iOS builds and App Store distribution)
- Google Play Developer account (for Android builds and Play Store distribution)
