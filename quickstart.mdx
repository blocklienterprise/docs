## Step 1 — Install the WordPress plugin

1. Download the **Blockli Mobile App** plugin zip from your Studio account.
2. In your WordPress admin, go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**.
3. Upload the zip and click **Activate**.
4. A **Blockli Mobile App** menu item appears in your WordPress sidebar.

<Note>
  BuddyBoss Platform must be installed and active before activating Blockli.
</Note>

## Step 2 — Create your app and connect WordPress

You can do this in either order.

**Option A — Create in Studio first:**

1. Log in to Studio and click **New App**. Enter your app name and click **Create**.
2. In your WordPress admin, go to **Blockli Mobile App → Overview** and click **Connect**.
3. You'll be redirected to Studio. Select the app you just created — your WordPress site is connected automatically.

**Option B — Connect from WordPress directly:**

1. In your WordPress admin, go to **Blockli Mobile App → Overview** and click **Connect**.
2. You'll be redirected to Studio. Create a new app there and enter your app name.
3. The app and connection are set up in one step.

See [Creating Your First App](/studio/create-app) for more detail.

## Step 3 — Configure your app

In Studio, go to **App Config** and set up:

- **Branding** — app icon, splash screen, and brand colors
- **Navigation** — which tabs appear in the bottom tab bar and what goes in the More menu
- **Modules** — Activity, Groups, Forums, Courses, Podcasts, and other features

## Step 4 — Add store credentials

Before you can build, you need credentials for each platform.

**iOS:** Go to **Store Setup → Credentials → iOS** and add your App Store Connect API key, APNs auth key, and bundle identifiers. See [iOS Credentials](/studio/credentials-ios).

**Android:** Go to **Store Setup → Credentials → Android** and upload your Firebase service account and set your package names. See [Android Credentials](/studio/credentials-android).

## Step 5 — Build your app

Go to **Build** in Studio and click **New Build**. Choose your platform and build profile.

### Build profiles

| Profile | What it is | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Preview** | A test app using your test bundle identifier | Ongoing testing — install directly on your device without TestFlight or the Play Store |
| **Release** | Your production app | When you're ready to submit to the App Store or Google Play |
| **Development** | A development build using the Expo Dev client | For custom development work on the app binary |

Builds typically take 10–20 minutes. You'll see status updates in the Build tab as the job progresses.

## Step 6 — Test your app

**We recommend keeping a dedicated Preview app installed on your device at all times.** It uses your test bundle identifier, so it installs alongside your production app and can be updated freely.

**iOS testing options:**

- **Preview build** — install the `.ipa` directly on your device without going through TestFlight. Great for quick iteration.
- **Release build → TestFlight** — from a completed release build, click **Submit to TestFlight** in Studio. Use TestFlight to test the production build before submitting it for App Store review.

## Step 7 — Submit to the App Store and Google Play

From a completed release build, click **Submit to Store**. See [Store Submission](/studio/store-submission) for the full checklist.
