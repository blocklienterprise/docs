Android needs a **Firebase project** (app registration and push notifications) and, optionally, a **Google Play service account** for automated Play Store uploads.

In Studio, go to **Store Setup → Credentials → Android**.

## 1. Connect Firebase

### Create a Firebase project

Already have a Firebase project? Skip to **Get the service account JSON**.

<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="https://blockli-app-videos.b-cdn.net/firebase_admin_sdk_setup.mp4" />

1. Go to [console.firebase.google.com](https://console.firebase.google.com) and sign in with your Google account.
2. Click **Create a project** (it may say **Add project**).
3. Enter a name, such as your app or company name. Firebase generates a project ID you can edit.
4. Continue past any AI assistance screen, then choose whether to enable Google Analytics. Studio doesn't need it.
5. Click **Create project**, wait for setup to finish, then click **Continue**.

You can also use an existing Google Cloud project, such as the one you use for Play. A separate Firebase project works fine.

### Get the service account JSON

<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="https://blockli-app-videos.b-cdn.net/adding-service-json-for-existing-app.mp4" />

1. Click the **gear icon** next to **Project Overview**, then **Project settings**.
2. Open the **Service accounts** tab.
3. Make sure **Firebase Admin SDK** is selected, then click **Generate new private key**.
4. Confirm with **Generate key**. A JSON file downloads. Google keeps no copy, so store it securely.

### Add it to Studio

1. In the Android card, find **Service account (admin SDK) JSON** and click **Upload service account JSON**.
2. Click **Save & verify**.

You should see **✓ Connected to Firebase — N Android apps found**. A new project shows 0 apps.

<Note>
  This service account lets Studio register your Android apps and relay Firebase Cloud Messaging v1 push notifications.
</Note>

## 2. Set your package names

Package names are set on the **Blockli project** tab, in the **Android** section. You need a **release** package name and a **test** package name.

### Use an app that already exists in Firebase

Open **Release package name** or **Test package name**. Studio lists the Android apps in your Firebase project. Type to search, then pick one.

### Register new apps

1. Open either Android field and choose **Register new…**.
2. In the side panel, enter an **App nickname** and your **Release package name** (for example `com.yourcompany.yourapp`).
3. Studio suggests the **Test package name** by adding `.test` to the release name. You can edit it.
4. Review the preview, then click **Create in Firebase**.

Studio registers both apps in your Firebase project, generates the matching `google-services.json`, and fills in both fields.

Click **Save** at the bottom of the tab when you're done.

## `google-services.json` (optional)

Studio creates and saves `google-services.json` automatically when you register apps, so **you normally don't need to upload one**.

If you already have a `google-services.json` you want to use, upload it under **google-services.json** on the Android card. Get it from Firebase → **Project settings → Your apps**.

## 3. Google Play service account (optional)

Only needed for automatic Play Store uploads. If you upload builds to Play by hand, skip this.

<Note>
  Create the app in Play Console yourself before your first upload. Google doesn't allow apps to be created through the API.
</Note>

### Create the service account

  <iframe src="https://player.mediadelivery.net/embed/757449/a33fd1d3-7755-4a9e-bfe8-c509fc8d5f61?autoplay=false&loop=false&muted=true&preload=true&responsive=true" loading="lazy" style={{border:0, position:'absolute', top:0, left:0, height:'100%', width:'100%'}} allow="accelerometer;gyroscope;autoplay;encrypted-media;picture-in-picture;fullscreen;" allowFullScreen={true} />
</div>

1. Open [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com) and sign in with the Google account that owns your Play Console, or one with access to the project.
2. Pick or create a project. Then go to **APIs & Services → Library**, search for **Google Play Android Developer API**, and click **Enable**.
3. Go to **IAM & Admin → Service Accounts** and click **\+ Create service account**.
4. Enter a name such as `blockli-play-publisher`. Add a description if you like. Click **Create and continue**.
5. Skip the next two steps ("Grant this service account access to the project" and "Grant users access to this service account") and click **Done**. Play permissions are set in Play Console, not here.
6. Copy the new account's email. It looks like `blockli-play-publisher@your-project.iam.gserviceaccount.com`.

### Download the JSON key

1. In **IAM & Admin → Service Accounts**, click the account you created.
2. Open the **Keys** tab and click **Add key → Create new key**.
3. Choose **JSON** and click **Create**. The file downloads. Google keeps no copy, so store it securely.

<Note>
  If **Create new key** is greyed out, your organization blocks service account key creation (the `iam.disableServiceAccountKeyCreation` policy). Ask your Google Workspace admin to allow it.
</Note>

### Give it access in Play Console

1. Open Play Console and go to **Users and permissions → Invite new users**.
2. Paste the service account's email.
3. Grant access to your app, or account-level permissions. Uploading builds needs release rights, or Admin.
4. Click **Invite user**. Service accounts have no inbox, so there's nothing to accept.

Access can take a while to become active. If an upload fails with a permissions error right after inviting, wait and try again.

### Add it to Studio

1. In the Android card, find **Google Play service account (.json)** and click **Upload Play .json**.
2. Click **Save**.

Studio only stores this file. There's no connection check, so you'll find out it works on your first Play upload.
