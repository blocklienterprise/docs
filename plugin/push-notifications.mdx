Blockli apps support push notifications on both iOS and Android. Notifications can include **images** alongside the title and message.

To enable push notifications, you need to upload your Firebase and APNs credentials in Studio first. See [iOS Credentials](/studio/credentials-ios) and [Android Credentials](/studio/credentials-android).

You manage push in WordPress under **Blockli → Push**, which has three tabs: **Send**, **Scheduled**, and **History**.

## Send a push

On the **Send** tab, choose who receives it:

- **All registered devices**
- **Specific users**
- **Filter members**: access groups, profile types, groups, courses, and memberships, with a count of how many can receive it

Write a title and message, and optionally add an image and a link the push opens. Click **Send push**.

The tab also shows delivery stats: registered devices, deliveries in the last 24 hours, and the last delivery.

## Schedule a push

Tick **Send later** and pick a date and time, in your site's time zone and up to 90 days ahead. The button changes to **Schedule push**.

Who receives it is worked out when it's sent, so members who match your filters by then are included.

Scheduled pushes appear on the **Scheduled** tab, with a counter on the tab while any are waiting. For each one you can:

- **Edit** — change the text, image, link, audience, or time.
- **Cancel** — it won't be sent, and stays in the list as canceled.

## History

The **History** tab lists every push sent in the last 30 days, one line per push, newest first: when it was sent, the title and message, who sent it, and how many were delivered and failed. Click a line to see each device it went to, with the reason for any failure.

Filter by who sent it (admin, AI agent, scheduled, automatic, or tests), show only pushes with failures, or search the title and message. Delivery details older than 30 days are removed automatically.

## Automatic notifications

Automatic notifications are triggered by activity on your platform — no manual action needed. These include:

- **BuddyBoss platform notifications** — new messages, group invites, connection requests, activity mentions, comments, forum replies, and more
- **Custom notifications** — any additional notification events integrated into the plugin

Members can manage which notifications they receive from the notification preferences screen in the app.

## Push notifications from AI agents

With the [AI App Builder](/ai/overview), agents can preview, send, and schedule pushes after you allow it. See [Push notifications by chat](/ai/push).
