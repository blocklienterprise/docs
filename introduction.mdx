Blockli connects three pieces:

| Component | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **WordPress Plugin** | Installed on your site. Connects your community data, authentication, and push notifications to Studio. |
| **Studio Cloud** | The web dashboard where you configure, build, and publish your app. Controls branding, navigation, credentials, and live updates. |
| **Mobile App** | The white-label native app delivered to your members on iOS and Android. Powered by your WordPress site, configured by Studio. |

## How it works

1. **Install the plugin** on your WordPress / BuddyBoss site.
2. **Connect Studio** to your site from the plugin's Overview page.
3. **Configure your app** in Studio — branding, navigation, and screens.
4. **Add credentials** for the iOS App Store and Google Play.
5. **Build and publish** your app to the App Store and Google Play.
6. **Push live updates** any time you change configuration or content — no new App Store release required.

## Requirements

- WordPress 5.8\+, PHP 7.4\+
- **BuddyBoss Platform** installed and activated
- A Blockli Studio account
- Apple Developer account (for iOS builds and App Store distribution)
- Google Play Developer account (for Android builds and Play Store distribution)
